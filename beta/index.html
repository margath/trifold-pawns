🔒 Privacy

This tool collects nothing about you. Everything below is a plain-language description of how it actually behaves, not a legal document.

✅ Your work never leaves your device

Images, pawn names, stat blocks and action cards are all processed in your browser. None of it is uploaded anywhere. Saving a project writes a .json file to your own computer, and only when you click Save.

✅ No accounts, analytics, cookies or tracking

There is no sign-in, no analytics script, and no advertising. The app sets no cookies and writes nothing to browser storage — closing the tab without saving genuinely discards your work.

✅ No third-party requests

The page loads no external scripts, fonts, or images — everything is embedded in the file itself. The only thing it asks the server for is its own starter project and, if installed, its own offline copy.

✅ Offline copies stay local

Installing this as an app stores copies of its own files on your device so it works without a connection. Clearing site data for this site removes them.

⚠️ Hosting — Cloudflare

This site is served through Cloudflare. As the host and CDN, Cloudflare may process technical connection data when delivering the page to you — typically your IP address, the time of the request, your browser's user-agent, and security or bot-mitigation signals.

That processing is Cloudflare's, not ours; we never see it, and it happens for any site they serve. Their handling of it is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

⚠️ Links that leave this page

Links to whitebeard.blog, Ko-fi and tools.gameportal.win are ordinary links. Nothing is sent to those sites unless you click, and once there their own policies apply.

If you self-host this file, the hosting section above is replaced by whatever your own host does. Everything else stays true, because it is a property of the file rather than the server.
